# RoadGain privacy policy

Effective: 2026-08-29

RoadGain has no account, ads, analytics, cloud sync, or Internet permission. In adaptive
mode it uses Android location services only to read the current speed while the visible
foreground service is running. Coordinates and route history are not logged, transmitted,
or persisted. Stored data is limited to user-created gain profiles, speed-curve settings,
and display preferences on the device.

Stopping adaptive mode immediately requests removal of location updates. Uninstalling the
app removes its stored settings. Android or Google Play services may independently process
location according to the device owner's platform settings and policies.

Extra audio gain can clip sound, damage speakers, mask traffic cues, and harm hearing.
RoadGain does not measure acoustic output and cannot certify any gain setting as safe.
